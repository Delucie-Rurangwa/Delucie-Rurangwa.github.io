About me

My interest in software started with a simple question: why are so few digital tools built for the languages, data and realities of African communities?

I specialise in Machine Learning, and most of my work sits where backend engineering meets data. I’ve fine-tuned a transformer to classify news in Kirundi, built a web app to organise station data during my internship at Spiro Rwanda, and I’m building a payment gateway in Java from the ground up.

My goal is to become a Data Engineer, building the reliable data foundations that let AI serve sectors like energy, health and language technology in Rwanda.
